## Combine design (justification)
- No sample sizes -> can't weight by N. SF6 population pyramid: HighM > GrandM > UltM, so UltM is smallest-sample/noisiest (confirmed: UltM spikes A.K.I 5.399, Blanka 5.287).
- Tiers: weight HighM:GrandM:UltM = 3:2:1 (population-descending). Flag rows where tier spread is large (low confidence).
- Months: equal-average for the LEVEL (max sample, smooth noise) + report Mar->May slope separately for DIRECTION (meta drift visible, not baked in).

## Sub rec method (complementary, weight extremes, discount 五分)
Full matrix pulled at GrandM(41) x 3 months. For each candidate sub:
  coverage = sum over Terry's opponents O of  w(O) * (sub_value_vs_O - 5.0)
  where w(O) = (5.0 - terry_value_vs_O) clipped to >0  => only Terry's BAD matchups count,
  weighted by how bad. Extreme weighting (square the weight) to discount near-五分.
  Penalty if sub ALSO bad vs O (shared weakness).
  Anti-correlation cross-check: corr(sub row, terry row) — want negative.
Exclude Ingrid as candidate and as opponent.
